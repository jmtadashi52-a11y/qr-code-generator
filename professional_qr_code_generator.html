Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about generating and using QR codes.

Are these QR codes free for commercial use?

Yes! All QR codes generated on QRify Pro are completely free, perpetual, and without any expiration or scan limits. You can use them on menus, products, advertisements, and websites freely.

Will my QR code expire over time?

No. Since we generate static QR codes directly encoded with your provided text, URL, or data string, they will remain active forever as long as your destination link or content remains online.

What is error correction level?

Error correction allows the QR code to remain scannable even if it is partially damaged, dirty, or covered by a logo. Higher levels add more data redundancy at the cost of denser QR patterns.

Privacy & Data Security Policy

At QRify Pro, your privacy is our utmost priority. Unlike online tools that store your sensitive credentials, Wi-Fi passwords, phone numbers, and vCard details on remote database servers, all QR generation happens entirely client-side inside your web browser using sandboxed JavaScript.

We do not collect, log, or share any information you input into the generator fields. You can generate codes with complete confidence and peace of mind.
