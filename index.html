QR Code Generator FAQ

Answers to common questions about QRifyPro.

Are QRifyPro QR codes free to use?

QRifyPro does not charge a fee for generating QR codes. You are responsible for ensuring that your use of the encoded content complies with applicable laws and third-party terms.

Do static QR codes expire?

A static QR code does not have an expiration date built into the QR code itself. If it contains a URL, however, the QR code will only continue to work if the destination URL remains available.

What is QR error correction?

Error correction adds redundancy to a QR code so it can remain readable when part of the code is damaged or obscured. Higher correction levels generally make the QR pattern denser.

Does QRifyPro store my QR content?

QR generation is performed in your browser and the current generator does not send your QR content to a QRifyPro application server. However, the website uses third-party services for hosting and external resources, which may process technical information according to their own policies.
